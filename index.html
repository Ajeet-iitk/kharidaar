    Wonders of the bazaar

    Small experiments hidden in the market. Pull a lever — every card does something.

    01 🌗

    The living sky

    Dawn, noon, dusk and a lamp-lit night — painted live on canvas, complete with birds, stars and sleepy streets.

    02 ⏳

    Time machine

    Play 24 hours in 30 seconds. Watch the shoppers go home, the lamps wake up, and the sky change its mind.

    03 🧮

    The fair-price lens

    ₹250 for 250 g and ₹120 a kilo shouldn’t be compared raw. Kharidaar normalises every note to ₹/kg, ₹/L or ₹/piece.

    04 🧾

    The receipt forge

    Fill a basket, then forge a kirana-style bill — including how much your basket has inflated since its first note.

    05 🗣️

    Three tongues

    English, हिन्दी, or a comfortably bilingual EH. The whole bazaar changes language, including the fruit.

    06 🎵

    The sound garden

    No audio files, just maths: a warm procedural raga-ish loop, cart clacks, and tiny clicks for every stall.

    07 🚶

    NCP, but make it bazaar

    Non-character players wander the lanes with their own opinions about apple prices. Tap one to hear the gossip.

    08 ⌨️

    Shortcut garden

    Tap / to search, B for basket, T for theme, M for music, Esc to close. Try not to press them all at once.

    3D 09 🛒

    The walkable bazaar

    Step through the arch into a real 3D bazaar: shelves of goods, shopkeepers, wandering shoppers, and a trolley that follows you.

    About this place

    A small monument to everyday prices.

    “Kharidaar” means buyer. This bazaar is built from a real spreadsheet kept between April 2025 and November 2026 — apples in Delhi, litchi in Ghazipur, a bus to Varanasi, one tetanus shot, one haircut.

    Nothing here is for sale. There is no checkout, no affiliate link, no “add to cart” that ships anything. It is a price diary you can walk through, because memory is a better storefront than a sale.
